The Ramayana is traditionally divided into seven distinct books or chapters,
 known as Kandas. These Kandas chronologically trace the life of Lord Rama, 
 moving from his miraculous birth to his eventual ascension and departure from the mortal world.
 Here is the detailed breakdown of the seven Kandas of the epic:1. Bala Kanda (The Book of Youth)
 This book focuses on the early life, divine origins, and education of Rama and his brothers.Key Events:
  King Dasharatha performs the Putrakameshthi sacrifice to obtain children, resulting in the birth of Rama,
   Lakshmana, Bharata, and Shatrughna.Major Milestones: The sage Vishwamitra takes the young prince Rama and his brother 
   Lakshmana into the forest to fight predatory demons like Taraka. They travel to Mithila, where Rama strings the heavy bow of Shiva,
    winning the princess Sita in marriage.2. Ayodhya Kanda (The Book of Ayodhya)This chapter details the political turmoil and emotional
     crisis within the royal court of Ayodhya.Key Events: King Dasharatha decides to crown Rama as his successor, causing joy across
      the kingdom. However, Queen Kaikeyi is manipulated by her maid Manthara.Major Milestones: Kaikeyi uses two ancient boons to demand
       the banishment of Rama for 14 years and the coronation of her son, Bharata. Rama voluntarily accepts exile to protect his father's honor.
        Sita and Lakshmana join him, leaving the city in deep mourning, which leads to Dasharatha's death from heartbreak.
